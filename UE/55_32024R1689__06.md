<a id="d1e2090-1-1"></a>Article&nbsp;3

<a id="art_3.tit_1"></a>

Definitions

For the purposes of this Regulation, the following definitions apply:

</colgroup><tbody>
<tr>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">(1)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">‘AI system’ means a&nbsp;machine-based system that is designed to operate with varying levels of autonomy and that may exhibit adaptiveness after deployment, and that, for explicit or implicit objectives, infers, from the input it receives, how to generate outputs such as predictions, content, recommendations, or decisions that can influence physical or virtual environments;</p>
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

</tr>

</table>

<a id="art_4"></a>

<a id="d1e2795-1-1"></a>Article&nbsp;4

<a id="art_4.tit_1"></a>

AI literacy

Providers and deployers of AI systems shall take measures to ensure, to their best extent, a&nbsp;sufficient level of AI literacy of their staff and other persons dealing with the operation and use of AI systems on their behalf, taking into account their technical knowledge, experience, education and training and the context the AI systems are to be used in, and considering the persons or groups of persons on whom the AI systems are to be used.
