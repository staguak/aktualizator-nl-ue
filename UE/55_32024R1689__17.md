<a id="d1e38-144-1"></a>ANNEX&nbsp;XIII

Criteria for the designation of general-purpose AI models with systemic risk referred to in Article&nbsp;51

For the purpose of determining that a&nbsp;general-purpose AI model has capabilities or an impact equivalent to those set out in Article&nbsp;51(1), point (a), the Commission shall take into account the following criteria:

<table width="100%" border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0">
<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">
</colgroup><tbody>
<tr>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">(a)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">the number of parameters of the model;</p>
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table width="100%" border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0">
<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">
</colgroup><tbody>
<tr>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">(b)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">the quality or size of the data set, for example measured through tokens;</p>
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table width="100%" border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0">
<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">
</colgroup><tbody>
<tr>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">(c)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">the amount of computation used for training the model, measured in floating point operations or indicated by a&nbsp;combination of other variables such as estimated cost of training, estimated time required for the training, or estimated energy consumption for the training;</p>
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table width="100%" border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0">
<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">
</colgroup><tbody>
<tr>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">(d)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">the input and output modalities of the model, such as text to text (large language models), text to image, multi-modality, and the state of the art thresholds for determining high-impact capabilities for each modality, and the specific type of inputs and outputs (e.g.&nbsp;biological sequences);</p>
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table width="100%" border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0">
<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">
</colgroup><tbody>
<tr>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">(e)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">the benchmarks and evaluations of capabilities of the model, including considering the number of tasks without additional training, adaptability to learn new, distinct tasks, its level of autonomy and scalability, the tools it has access to;</p>
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table width="100%" border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0">
<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">
</colgroup><tbody>

<p class="oj-normal">(f)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">

</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
