<a id="d1e32-87-1"></a>Corrigendum to Regulation (EC) No 593/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 June 2008 on the law applicable to contractual obligations (Rome I)

‘This Regulation shall apply to contracts concluded after 17 December 2009.’,

‘This Regulation shall apply to contracts concluded as from 17 December 2009.’.
