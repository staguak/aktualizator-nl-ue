            REGULATION (EU) 2026/2359 OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL

of 7&nbsp;October 2026

amending Regulation (EC) No&nbsp;883/2004 on the coordination of social security systems and Regulation (EC) No&nbsp;987/2009 laying down the procedure for implementing Regulation (EC) No&nbsp;883/2004

(Text with relevance for the EEA and Switzerland)

<a id="pbl_1"></a>

THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

<a id="cit_1"></a>

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article&nbsp;48 thereof,

<a id="cit_2"></a>

Having regard to the proposal from the European Commission,

<a id="cit_3"></a>

After transmission of the draft legislative act to the national Parliaments,

<a id="cit_4"></a>

Having regard to the opinion of the European Economic and Social Committee&nbsp;<a id="ntc1-L_202602359EN.000101-E0001" href="51_32026R2359__04.md#ntr1-L_202602359EN.000101-E0001">(<span class="oj-super oj-note-tag">1</span>)</a>,

<a id="cit_5"></a>

Having regard to the opinion of the Committee of the Regions&nbsp;<a id="ntc2-L_202602359EN.000101-E0002" href="51_32026R2359__04.md#ntr2-L_202602359EN.000101-E0002">(<span class="oj-super oj-note-tag">2</span>)</a>,

<a id="cit_6"></a>

Acting in accordance with the ordinary legislative procedure&nbsp;<a id="ntc3-L_202602359EN.000101-E0003" href="51_32026R2359__04.md#ntr3-L_202602359EN.000101-E0003">(<span class="oj-super oj-note-tag">3</span>)</a>,

There is a&nbsp;need to develop a&nbsp;stable legal framework appropriate to long-term care benefits within that Regulation, continuing coordination as sickness benefits as a&nbsp;general rule and including a&nbsp;clear definition and a&nbsp;list of such benefits.</p>

<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">In order to ensure a&nbsp;real link between the unemployed person and the labour market of the Member State providing unemployment benefits, the rules on the aggregation of periods for the purpose of conferring an entitlement to unemployment benefits should, with the exception of wholly unemployed cross-border workers as referred to in Article&nbsp;65(2), (3) and&nbsp;(5) of Regulation (EC) No&nbsp;883/2004, be subject to the condition that the insured person has most recently completed an uninterrupted period of at least one month of insurance, employment or self-employment in that Member State, and has thereby contributed to the financing of the unemployment benefit scheme of that Member State over a&nbsp;predefined period. Where no such period has been completed in the Member State of most recent insurance, employment or self-employment, the previous Member State in which that person completed a&nbsp;period of insurance, employment or self-employment should become competent, provided that the person completed an uninterrupted period of at least one month in that Member State. Registration with the employment services of the Member State of most recent insurance should have the same effect as registration with the employment services of the Member State in which the unemployed person had been previously insured. Where the person did not complete the required period in either of those Member States, the Member State of most recent insurance, employment or self-employment should become competent.</p>

<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">Unemployed persons who during their most recent activity resided in a&nbsp;Member State other than the competent Member State and who continue to reside in that other Member State or return there should receive benefits in accordance with the legislation of the competent Member State, provided that they have, in the Member State of their most recent activity, completed uninterrupted periods of insurance, employment or self-employment of at least&nbsp;22 weeks (‘affiliation period’). Only uninterrupted periods of insurance, employment or self-employment completed under the legislation of the competent Member State should be taken into account for the purpose of calculating the affiliation period. Subject to completion of the affiliation period, it is for the competent Member State to assess whether the person meets the conditions for entitlement to unemployment benefits laid down in its national legislation, as if the person were residing in that Member State. The person should comply with the obligations laid down in the applicable legislation of the competent Member State. If the person decides to make him or herself available to the employment services of another Member State in order to seek work there, he or she should comply with the conditions laid down in the legislation of, and be subject to the control procedure organised in, that other Member State. The institution in the Member State to which the unemployed person has gone should provide the competent Member State, on a&nbsp;monthly basis, with relevant information concerning the follow-up to the unemployed person’s situation, in particular whether he or she is still registered with the employment services and is complying with organised checking, and activation or job placement procedures.</p>

<p class="oj-normal">(11)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">The labour market in Luxembourg is characterised by a&nbsp;number of specific features. Frontier workers make up a&nbsp;very significant proportion of the total working population in Luxembourg, which greatly exceeds the proportion of frontier workers in the working population of other Member States. The introduction of the new rules relating to frontier and cross-border workers is expected to place a&nbsp;very high administrative burden on the Luxembourg public employment authority, due to a&nbsp;significant increase in the number of frontier workers for whom it would become the competent institution. It is therefore appropriate to grant Luxembourg an additional phasing-in period, during which Articles&nbsp;65 and 86 of Regulation (EC) No&nbsp;883/2004 and Articles&nbsp;56 and 70 of, and Annex&nbsp;5 to, Regulation (EC) No&nbsp;987/2009, in the version in force before 1&nbsp;November 2026, continue to apply, in order to allow the time required to take all necessary preparatory steps for a&nbsp;smooth transition from the current regulatory framework to the new rules and to adjust the social security system. During that phasing-in period, Article&nbsp;56a of Regulation (EC) No&nbsp;987/2009, which is introduced by this Regulation, should not apply to Luxembourg.</p>

<p class="oj-normal">Effective recovery is a&nbsp;means of preventing and tackling fraud and abuse and ensuring the smooth functioning of social security schemes. The recovery procedures provided for in Chapter&nbsp;III of Title&nbsp;IV of Regulation (EC) No&nbsp;987/2009 are based on the procedures and rules provided for in Council Directive 2008/55/EC&nbsp;<a id="ntc11-L_202602359EN.000101-E0011" href="51_32026R2359__04.md#ntr11-L_202602359EN.000101-E0011">(<span class="oj-super oj-note-tag">11</span>)</a>. That Directive has been superseded by Council Directive 2010/24/EU&nbsp;<a id="ntc12-L_202602359EN.000101-E0012" href="51_32026R2359__04.md#ntr12-L_202602359EN.000101-E0012">(<span class="oj-super oj-note-tag">12</span>)</a>, which introduced a&nbsp;uniform instrument to be used for enforcement measures as well as a&nbsp;standard form for the notification of instruments and measures relating to claims. In a&nbsp;review by the Administrative Commission, most Member States found it advantageous to use a&nbsp;uniform instrument for enforcement similar to that provided for by Directive 2010/24/EU. It is therefore necessary that the rules for mutual assistance in recovery of social security claims reflect the new measures in Directive&nbsp;2010/24/EU, in order to ensure more effective recovery and a&nbsp;smooth functioning of the coordination rules.</p>
