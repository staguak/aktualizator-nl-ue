<a id="art_1"></a>

<a id="d1e871-1-1"></a>Article&nbsp;1

<a id="art_1.tit_1"></a>

Subject matter

This Regulation:

<table width="100%" border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0">
<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">
</colgroup><tbody>
<tr>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">(a)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">lays down rules on containment, use, recovery, recycling, reclamation and destruction of fluorinated greenhouse gases and on related ancillary measures, such as certification and training, which includes the safe handling of fluorinated greenhouse gases and of alternative substances that are not fluorinated;</p>
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">
</colgroup><tbody>

<p class="oj-normal">(b)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">imposes conditions on the production, import, export, placing on the market, subsequent supply and use of fluorinated greenhouse gases, and of specific products and equipment containing fluorinated greenhouse gases or whose functioning relies upon those gases;</p>
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table width="100%" border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0">
<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">

</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

</td>

</td>

<a id="art_3"></a>

<a id="d1e931-1-1"></a>Article&nbsp;3

<a id="art_3.tit_1"></a>

Definitions

For the purposes of this Regulation, the following definitions apply:

<table width="100%" border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0">
<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">
</colgroup><tbody>
<tr>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">(1)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">‘global warming potential’ or ‘GWP’ means the climatic warming potential of a greenhouse gas relative to that of carbon dioxide (CO<span class="oj-sub">2</span>), calculated in terms of the 100-year global warming potential, unless otherwise specified, of one kilogram of a greenhouse gas relative to one kilogram of CO<span class="oj-sub">2</span>, as set out in Annexes&nbsp;I, II, III and VI or in the case of mixtures, calculated in accordance with Annex&nbsp;VI;</p>
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table width="100%" border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0">
<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">
</colgroup><tbody>
<tr>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">(2)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">‘mixture’ means a substance composed of two or more substances, at least one of which is a substance listed in Annex&nbsp;I, II or III;</p>
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table width="100%" border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0">
<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">
</colgroup><tbody>
<tr>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">(3)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">‘tonne of CO<span class="oj-sub">2</span> equivalent’ means a quantity of greenhouse gases expressed as the product of the weight of the greenhouse gases in metric tonnes and of their global warming potential;</p>
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table width="100%" border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0">
<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">
</colgroup><tbody>
<tr>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">(4)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">‘hydrofluorocarbons’ or ‘HFCs’ means the substances listed in Section 1 of Annex&nbsp;I or mixtures containing any of those substances;</p>
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table width="100%" border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0">
<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">
</colgroup><tbody>
<tr>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">(5)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">‘operator‘ means the undertaking exercising actual power over the technical functioning of products, equipment or facilities covered by this Regulation, or the owner designated by a Member State as being responsible for the operator’s obligations in specific cases;</p>
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table width="100%" border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0">
<colgroup><col width="4%">
<col width="96%">
</colgroup><tbody>
<tr>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">(6)</p>
</td>
<td valign="top">
<p class="oj-normal">‘placing on the market’ means the customs release for free circulation in the Union or the supplying or making available to another person within the Union, for the first time, for payment or free of charge, or the use of substances produced, or of products or equipment manufactured, for own use;</p>
</td>
</tr>
