<a id="id-73b73288-906a-488f-8ce7-a2e7fbc32911"></a>17.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Outdoor workplaces (special provisions)

When workers are employed at workstations outdoors, such workstations must as far as possible be organized so that workers:

(a)

are protected against inclement weather conditions and if necessay against falling objects;

(b)

are not exposed to harmful noise levels nor to harmful
                     <a href="http://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/31989L0654R%2801%29" onclick="window.open(this.href,'_blank'); return false;" title="31989L0654R(01): REPLACED">
                        <span class="boldface">►C1</span>
                     </a>
                  &nbsp;outdoor&nbsp;◄  influences such as gases, vapours or dust;

(c)

are able to leave their workstations swiftly in the event of danger or are able to be rapidly assisted;

(d)

cannot slip or fall.
