<a id="d1e4062-1-1"></a>Article&nbsp;3

<a id="art_3.tit_1"></a>

Entry into force and date of application

This Regulation shall enter into force on the first day of the month following the date of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

<a id="fnp_1"></a>

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Strasbourg, 7&nbsp;October 2026.

For the European Parliament

The President

R. METSOLA

For the Council

The President

T. BYRNE
