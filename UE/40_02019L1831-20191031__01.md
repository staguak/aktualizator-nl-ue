<a id="id-34669d18-1154-4a08-b76c-4490c5ff81dc"></a>
               COMMISSION DIRECTIVE (EU) 2019/1831

<a id="id-39e72dc5-4cae-4f98-a8fe-9392b0d25097"></a>of 24 October 2019

<a id="id-a61ffaa7-afee-4f3f-972b-46b4abc22c65"></a>establishing a fifth list of indicative occupational exposure limit values pursuant to Council Directive 98/24/EC and amending Commission Directive 2000/39/EC

<a id="id-a4f30f26-4552-40df-97aa-50de2a7cff22"></a>(Text with EEA relevance)

<a id="enc_1"></a>

<a id="art_1"></a>

<a id="id-8f3c1fe2-c58d-412e-bca7-e08912a9ab3f"></a>Article 1

A fifth list of EU indicative occupational exposure limit values is established for the chemical agents listed in the Annex.

<a id="art_2"></a>

<a id="id-98ad4274-0eae-457f-addb-9fa30e92522b"></a>Article 2

Member States shall establish national occupational exposure limit values for the chemical agents listed in the Annex, taking into account the EU limit values.

<a id="art_3"></a>

<a id="id-c23ec1cf-ab72-4240-8b68-ef2eeedbcd88"></a>Article 3

In the Annex to Directive 2000/39/EC, the reference to cumene is deleted with effect from 20 May 2021.

<a id="art_4"></a>

<a id="id-71027f49-bfc9-4ce2-bdb3-1774b77a8da1"></a>Article 4

1.

The Member States shall adopt and publish, by 20 May 2021 at the latest, the laws, regulations and administrative provisions necessary to comply with this Directive.

They shall forthwith communicate to the Commission the text of those provisions and shall accompany their notification with one or more explanatory documents in the form of tables showing the correlation between the provisions and this Directive.

When Member States adopt these provisions, they shall contain a reference to this Directive or be accompanied by such a reference on the occasion of their official publication. Member States shall determine how such reference is to be made.

2.

Member States shall communicate to the Commission the text of the main national legal provisions which they adopt in the field covered by this Directive.

<a id="art_5"></a>

<a id="id-fa74d90b-0523-4bdc-9968-b9ac6cdb15bd"></a>Article 5

This Directive shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

<a id="art_6"></a>

<a id="id-d6ccf669-3e42-40b6-8c21-b26d6d7aecff"></a>Article 6

This Directive is addressed to the Member States.
