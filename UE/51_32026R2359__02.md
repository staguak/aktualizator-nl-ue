Long-term care benefits should therefore be interpreted accordingly. In particular, long-term care benefits should not include social or medical assistance. Benefits granted on a&nbsp;discretionary basis, after an individual assessment of the claimant’s personal needs, are not considered to be long-term care benefits for the purposes of this Regulation.</p>
